# DUKAN
AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Platform for Marginalized Artisans

From Local Artisans to a Wider Digital Market

📌 About the Project

DUKAN is a digital platform designed to help marginalized artisans showcase, catalog, and market their traditional products online.

Many artisans depend on local markets, exhibitions, and intermediaries to sell their products. This limits their market reach and reduces their opportunities to connect directly with customers.

DUKAN aims to provide artisans with a simple digital platform where they can:

Create and manage digital product catalogs
Showcase their traditional products
Reach customers beyond their local markets
Improve product visibility
Reduce dependency on intermediaries
Preserve and promote traditional art and craftsmanship
Access the platform in multiple languages
🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional and marginalized artisans often face challenges such as:

Limited access to digital marketplaces
Dependence on middlemen
Limited product visibility
Difficulty creating professional product catalogs
Restricted access to wider markets
Language and digital-literacy barriers
Limited opportunities to promote traditional crafts

DUKAN addresses these challenges by creating a user-friendly digital ecosystem for artisans.

💡 Our Solution

DUKAN combines digital cataloging, market linkage, multilingual accessibility, and AI-powered features into a single platform.

Core Features
🧑‍🎨 Artisan Profiles
Create digital profiles for artisans and showcase their work.
🛍️ Smart Product Catalog
Add products with images, descriptions, categories, prices, and other details.
🔎 Product Discovery
Help users discover traditional and handmade products.
🌐 Multilingual Support
Provide information in multiple Indian languages.
🤖 AI-Powered Features
Use AI to assist with product information, cataloging, recommendations, and other intelligent features.
📱 Mobile-Friendly Interface
Responsive design for smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
📊 Digital Market Linkage
Connect artisans with potential customers and broader markets.
🏺 Cultural Preservation
Digitally document and promote traditional crafts and artisan stories.
