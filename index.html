The Rural Reality & The ShilpSetu Solution

Great Craftsmanship. A Direct Digital Bridge.

India's rural artisan sector harbors centuries of heritage techniques, but traditional digital tools are too complex, cloud-dependent, and intermediary-driven. Here is how ShilpSetu transforms this reality.

✕

Traditional Barriers

01

Digital Literacy & Language Divide

Complex English-only interfaces with steep learning curves make commercial e-commerce inaccessible to rural artisans.

02

Manual Cataloging Bottleneck

Writing descriptions, assigning categories, and tagging materials requires tedious manual input that takes hours per product.

03

Exploitative Intermediaries

Artisans rely on local middlemen who take substantial profit margins while the creator receives minimal remuneration.

04

Spotty Village Connectivity

Cloud-only apps break in low-bandwidth rural belts, causing data loss and preventing product listing on the spot.

✓

The ShilpSetu Solution

01

Vernacular Voice & Visual First

Artisans simply speak in their mother tongue (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, etc.) and snap a photo; no keyboard typing required.

02

Automated Gemini Smart Cataloging

Multimodal AI generates structured titles, craft categories, materials, and captivating cultural stories in seconds.

03

Direct Verified Micro-Storefront

Every artisan gets a public digital storefront with verifiable GI provenance, allowing direct buyer connections with zero commission.

04

Zero-Internet SQLite Edge Cache

Listings save immediately to on-device storage. When network signals return, our sync engine commits everything to the cloud.

The 5-Step Journey

Capture → Understand → Create → Store → Connect

A streamlined five-stage pipeline designed specifically for rural artisans to turn a physical craft into a market-ready digital product with minimal typing.

01

Capture

The artisan snaps a craft photo or speaks a vernacular voice note.

02

Understand

Gemini AI analyzes visual textures, motifs, and voice context.

03

Create

Synthesizes structured title, craft story, category, and price range.

04

Store

Saved immediately to local SQLite storage with auto-sync capability.

05

Connect

Published to verified digital storefront with direct buyer linkage.

Engineering Foundation

Battle-Tested Technology Stack

Layer 1

Frontend

Flutter, Dart, GoRouter — Cross-platform mobile UI with declarative routing for high-performance native apps on Android & iOS.

Layer 2

Backend

Python, FastAPI, Uvicorn — High-throughput async API server powering AI pipelines, marketplace logic, and real-time data processing.

Layer 3

AI Integration

Gemini 2.5/Flash — State-of-the-art multimodal vision and vernacular language model for craft taxonomy, NLP, translation, and smart cataloging.

Layer 4

Cloud & Database

Firebase — Authentication, Firestore real-time database, and cloud file storage for scalable, serverless backend infrastructure.

Layer 5

Version Control

Git + GitHub — Distributed version control and collaborative repository management for team-based development workflows.

Layer 6

Deployment IDE

Antigravity IDE— Primary development and deployment environment for building, debugging, and publishing mobile applications.

Layer 7

Design / Prototype

Stitch / Figma-Style — UI/UX prototyping and design system tools for crafting pixel-perfect interfaces and interactive wireframes.
